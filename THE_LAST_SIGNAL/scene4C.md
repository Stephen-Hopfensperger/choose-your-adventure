# Behind the Barrier

The flood barriers closed with a heavy crash.
Then someone knocked from the other side.
"Wait! I'm still here!"
Alex froze. They had trapped Dr. Vale behind the door.
Morgan found a service hatch above them.
"We can go around," they said. "Or open it again."
