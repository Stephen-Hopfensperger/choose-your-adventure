# Letting Go

Alex lifted the safety cover and held the release switch.
Heavy impacts traveled through the floor.
The garden platform separated from the station. Roots pulled free, and the blue plants sank into darkness.
The alarms stopped.
"Oxygen production has ended," NORA warned.
Everyone hurried toward the rescue pod. Alex paused beside the archive terminal.
