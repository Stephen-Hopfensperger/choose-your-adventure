# Cold Water

Alex opened the cooling valves.
Cold seawater rushed through the garden pipes. The plants' glow softened.
Then a battery warning appeared.
"Temporary cooling only," NORA said. "We need the generator."
Morgan checked the map. The generator room was close, but the route was filling with water.
